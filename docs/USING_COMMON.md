# Make yourself at home

Common brings photographs, small updates, circles and conversations into one place. Sign in with your existing account or create one to start sharing.

## Find your people

Use **Search** in the top bar from any signed-in page. Control K on Windows/Linux or Command K on macOS opens the same search. Type a name, circle or page, move with the arrow keys, and press Enter to open it. Escape closes the search.

In **Your feed**, **The latest** shows the posts currently shared with you. **Following** narrows this to people whose follow requests have been accepted. Follow someone from their profile to begin building that feed.

## Share a moment

Choose **Create** or the prompt in your feed. Write up to 500 Unicode code points, attach a JPG, PNG or GIF, and choose who can see the post. You can drop an image onto the composer; the picker accepts images up to 10 MB.

Close the composer to keep browsing, then reopen it to continue. The draft stays in memory while the signed-in workspace remains open. Reloading the page or signing out clears it. Control/Command Enter submits; a failed request leaves your draft intact.

## Stay for the conversation

Open a post's photo or comments to bring the conversation forward. On wide screens, image posts put the photo beside the discussion. Narrow screens stack them. Closing the dialog returns to the list without replacing it.

Comments support text and images. The 250-code-point counter includes Arabic and emoji. Some combined emoji use several code points. Likes update immediately while a request is in flight and revert if it fails.

## Keep the good stuff

Use the bookmark beside a post to save it. **Saved posts** in the navigation opens your collection, ordered by when you saved each post. Tap the bookmark again to remove it.

Your saved list belongs to your account. Saving does not override an author's audience choice: if they stop sharing a post with you, it stops appearing in the collection. If they delete it, its saved reference is removed.

## Explore your common ground

Circles bring membership, group posts, conversations and events together. Messages keep private conversations in reach. Activity gathers follow requests, invitations and updates. On the sign-in page, the three photographic chapters are manually controlled; they do not autoplay.

The interface respects reduced-motion preferences and keeps native document scrolling. See [validation](VALIDATION.md) for the checks performed and remaining browser verification.
